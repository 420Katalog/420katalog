01 / ABOUT US

More than a label.
A whole state of mind.

420Katalog is a marijuana-inspired lifestyle brand for people who make their own lane. We bring together expressive apparel, music, and the everyday rituals that shape our culture.

Our mission is to create a home for originality: thoughtful designs, good sounds, and a community that values authenticity over fitting in. Every part of the brand is made to feel confident, creative, and unmistakably you.

We believe trust comes from keeping it real. That means clear communication, a consistent point of view, and a culture-first mindset in everything we put out.
